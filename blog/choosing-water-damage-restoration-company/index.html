How to Find the Right Restoration Company in Shreveport, LA

Home / Blog / How to Find the Right Restoration Company in Shreveport, LA

Picking a restoration company is a major decision for any Shreveport, LA owner dealing with water damage. Your out-of-pocket cost, the pace of the work, the thoroughness of the repair, and the odds of secondary trouble such as mold months later all depend on who you hire. Unfortunately, the restoration industry has its share of unqualified operators who may leave you with incomplete work, inflated bills, or worse. This guide helps you identify the qualities that separate excellent restoration companies from the rest.

Check for IICRC Certification

In the restoration industry, the leading credential comes from the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification (IICRC). To earn certification, a technician trains rigorously in restoration science, covering mold prevention, moisture measurement, structural drying, and proper extraction technique. Before hiring anyone, ask if the technicians carry current IICRC certifications in both Applied Structural Drying (ASD) and Water Damage Restoration (WRT). Steer clear of any firm unable to show proof of them.

Confirm Insurance and Licensing

Every restoration company working on your property should carry comprehensive insurance including general liability and workers' compensation. Request certificates of insurance and verify they're current. If the contractor is underinsured, an injury or further damage during the job could become your liability. Licensing matters too, so confirm the firm holds whatever business licenses Shreveport or Louisiana requires.

Check Availability and Arrival Speed

Water damage is a time-sensitive emergency. Each hour lost adds to both the damage and the bill. The restoration company you choose should offer true 24/7 emergency response with fast arrival times in your area. Find out their typical response time for Shreveport, and verify the emergency number goes to a real person, since an answering service or voicemail only adds delay.

Ask About Equipment and Process

Professional restoration requires professional equipment. Ask what type of extraction equipment the company uses (truck-mounted units are superior to portable-only operations), what type of dehumidifiers they deploy (LGR units are the industry standard), and whether they use thermal imaging for moisture detection. Ask how drying is monitored too, because a reputable company checks moisture every day and repositions equipment according to the readings instead of setting it up and walking away.

Check Reviews and References

A company's history shows up clearly in its online reviews. Look at the BBB, Yelp, and Google, and watch for steady patterns in quality, professionalism, and how satisfied customers were. Pay particular attention to reviews that mention the company's communication, timeliness, insurance assistance, and the final quality of restoration work. It is also fair to ask for the names of recent Shreveport water damage customers you can contact.

Get Clear on Prices and Insurance Billing

Reputable restoration companies provide detailed estimates before beginning work and can explain their pricing structure clearly. Good firms often price jobs with industry-standard software such as Xactimate, which insurers know and accept. Two good questions: does the firm work directly with insurers, and is direct billing to your carrier possible? Be wary of any company that wants a large payment up front or will not work with insurance.
