Floods start in many ways: heavy rain, storm surge, a river over its banks, a dead sump pump, a sewer backup or a major plumbing failure, and all of them call for fast professional help. Floodwater is almost always rated Category 2 (gray water) or Category 3 (black water), so it carries bacteria, contaminants and possibly hazardous material that can make people seriously ill. Day or night, 24/7, Shreveport Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout Shreveport to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

Cleanup opens with rapid extraction: truck-mounted units and submersible pumps that pull thousands of gallons an hour out of the building. After that, IICRC-certified technicians on our team measure how contaminated the area is, then sanitize it with antimicrobial products, HEPA-filtered air cleaning and targeted disinfection of all surfaces the water reached. Materials beyond saving, like contaminated drywall, damaged insulation and saturated carpet pad, are removed and disposed of the right way.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. Our crew returns daily, using thermal imaging and calibrated meters to track moisture until the structure is completely dry and mold growth is prevented. From start to finish we document each step for your insurance claim, with the moisture maps, equipment logs, photos and scope-of-work reports adjusters ask for.

How to Prevent Flood Cleanup Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Look into a flood insurance policy, including when your home sits outside a mapped flood zone
  • Store valuables and important papers off the basement floor in waterproof containers
  • A backwater valve on your sewer line keeps a backup from pushing sewage indoors
  • Make sure the ground slopes away from your foundation on every side
  • Roof runoff should leave the downspout far enough out that it cannot soak the foundation
  • Install a battery backup sump pump so you stay protected when storms knock out power

When to Call for Flood Cleanup in Shreveport

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water flooding out of a dishwasher, water heater or washing machine
  • A water line on walls or baseboards showing how high it rose
  • Living areas flooded by contaminated water from a backed-up drain or sewer
  • A sump pump that failed during heavy rain and let the basement flood
  • Water pooled on any floor of the building, no matter where it came from
  • Flooring that squelches when you walk on it and walls damp to the touch
  • Ground so saturated that puddles sit against the foundation walls

The Benefits of Professional Flood Cleanup

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Beyond the health concerns, a poor flood cleanup often leaves hidden moisture that turns into severe mold growth within days. Our technicians rely on psychrometric calculations, meter readings and thermal imaging to verify that each affected spot, from the framing to the subfloor to the inside of the walls, meets accepted drying standards. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.
